Quarterly Newsletter 4 – 2023

Dear Friends and Partners,

Visionful New Year greetings from the HOME of the FUTURE!

Here we are once again with our 4th and final newsletter for the year. You will find photos here showing all the events we have in this quarter.

In this episode, we would also like to feature our partners who have made things possible for us in 2023. So please read and be moved.

Visitors from Save the Children Global IT

December 22, 2023

Our quarter is greeted by visitors from Save the Children Global IT. They came to learn about Future Vision and what we do.

  • EVERISE Cares visited at the end of October and brought early Christmas gifts.

Save the Children

October 21, 2023

Save the Children STC has activities for children and of course, our Visionistas is part of the success of the projects of STC. At the same time, our Visionistas learned from all the activities they have with STC. Here are some of their comments.

On the 21st of October, we went to Paranaque Elementary School for an activity organized by Save the Children. My companions who participated included Kizhel, Jessica, Jessa, and Jesel. We had the main task of sticking circles and triangles onto a manila paper to represent our agreement or disagreement as young people with problems faced by individuals with a disability that the local government unit (LGU) will address.

I can say that we were stuck in more circles to show our agreement with their actions. They also provided us with delicious food, games that everyone enjoyed, and distributed certificates to honor our participation in the project. We were truly delighted to take part in such activities. Aside from participating in the task, we also learned much from them.

On a separate occasion, Save the Children took our Visionistas to the Manila Ocean Park. This is with American Express who sponsored the activity. Our Visionistas enjoyed and actively participated in the discussions after which they toured around.

Rejay got overly excited. He said, “Nakabuo po ako ng isang kakaibang family and friend relationship sa sa mga nakasamang personnel ng American Express.” He added, “Nagpapasalamat nga po ako dahil kahit hindi ko nakikita ang mga pinupuntahan namin ay inilalarawan naman po nila sa akin ang lahat.”

On October 25, Future Vision receives a plaque of appreciation from Save the Children at B Hotel in Quezon City.

Sensitivity Awareness with White Cane

December 2, 2023

The much-awaited “White Cane Day Celebration and the Sensitivity Awareness Day which were supposed to be scheduled for July and August, finally took place on December 2, 2023. This is the activity of PWDA in partnership with the Barangay for the beneficiaries of Future Vision.

We also took advantage of the film showing of Project ADAM. The most exciting activity on this day is the shopping of the participants using the fund of Three Hundred Pesos.

This is what Jesel has to say, “Ang experience ko sa panood ng Movie sa SM Megamall at pag-sha-shopping ay masaya ako dahil na-appreciate ko ang movie na pinanood. Nakatulog nga lang ako dahil sa sobrang lamig sa loob ng sinehan at inantok ako sa panonood. Isa rin sa nakapagpasaya sa akin ay ang pamimiling sama-sama sa SM. Masaya ako dahil nakabili ako ng regalo para sa aking sarili gamit ang ibinigay ng 300 PHP. Ang sarap umikot, mamasyal at mamili. Sana sa sunod mas maraming oras at may madagdag sa amount ng pambili.”

Jesel is one of the twenty-one blind participants who enjoyed roaming around and shopping. Thanks to PWDA of Bambang and the support of Capt. Ryan Esteban.

Our Plans, Dreams, and Needs!

Dreams

This year 2024, we are in our 10th year of operation. We plan to go out of Taguig City and reach more other blind and low-vision individuals in the nearby municipalities and cities like Pateros and Makati.

Of course, it is our dream to have a facility complete with all its amenities in every municipality and city that we have Future Vision.

It is also our dream to have equipment like a portable Braille embosser, for our reading materials, additional Braille Display, and additional desktop computers to aid both the blind teachers and the education of our members.

The following are some of the activities we plan to do in the year 2024:

  • Partnering with Pateros (Home Visitations) Future Vision-Pateros
  • Training for Teachers who are handling learners with Visual impairment.
  • Advanced computer Training for the blind and visually impaired
  • Economic Empowerment Through Massage Training (EETMT) a training for adult blind members to start a livelihood project (Massage Clinic)
  • Academic Subject training/enhancement particularly in Mathematics Science Technology and other practical Arts for the Blind.
  • Outdoor Fun And Learning Activities (OFALA)
  • Promote Arts, Music, and Sports Activities
  • And others
